## Bundled assets

Final entries are grouped by scene. Each entry records the creator, exact source page, download date, license, modifications, and SHA-256 of the bundled file. Hashes in this ledger must be regenerated whenever an asset changes.

No asset may ship unless its complete entry appears below and its license evidence has been reviewed.
